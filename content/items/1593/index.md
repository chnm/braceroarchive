Oftentimes, braceros were forced to show their hands to prove that they were experienced farmers. Inspectors would check to see how callused their hands were; according to them, the more callused the hands, the more experienced the farmer.At the Monterrey Processing Center, Mexico, an official checks a bracero's hands for calluses. Officials sought men with farm labor experience, verified by rough hands and strong muscles.
