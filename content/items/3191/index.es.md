03. Pictured: Rosa Castruita (Martinez) picking figs in Planada, California, circa 1947.  Married names are shown in parenthesis.<br/>
<br/>
The Castruita and Martinez families of Baldwin Park, and Claremont, California picked fruit alongside Braceros both locally in the San Gabriel Valley, and as migrant workers throughout California’s Central Valley.  <br/>
<br/>
*Daniel Martinez (photographer) is author of Thesis “The Impact of Bracero Programs on a Southern California Community”, pub. 1958 (also available on the Bracero Archive).<br/>
