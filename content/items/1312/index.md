Coming from Monterrey, Mexico the braceros walked over the bridge between Reynosa, Mexico and Hidalgo, Texas and crossed the Mexico-U.S. border. Right behind the border they were searched for contraband, sprayed with DDT and had to pass a physical examination at the Hidalgo Processing Center. <br/>
<br/>
Original Caption: Both busses and trains are used to bring the men to the reception center at the border for further processing by the American immigration and labor inspectors. Each night, 1200 men were loaded aboard a freight train --- 70 to each box car --- for an overnight ride from Monterrey, Mexico to Reynoso, a border town across from Hidalgo, Texas. The 150 mile trips takes 8 hours. (A, #10) [sic]Directly after the Mexicans are walked over the bridge from Mexico at Hidalgo, Texas, they are searched for contraband and are then sprayed with DDT. (B, #9)
