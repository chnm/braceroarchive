<p style="margin-bottom:0;color:#fff;margin:18px 36px 0;padding-bottom:24px;">The <em>Bracero History Archive</em> collects and makes available the oral histories and artifacts pertaining to the Bracero program, a guest worker initiative that spanned the years 1942-1964. Millions of Mexican agricultural workers crossed the border under the program to work in more than half of the states in America.</p>
